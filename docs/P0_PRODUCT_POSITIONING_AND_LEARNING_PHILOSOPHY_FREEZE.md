# P0 Product Positioning & Learning Philosophy Freeze

Status: Frozen

Purpose:

Freeze the highest-level product positioning, learning philosophy, and North Star principles for Video English Assistant.

This document is the upper-level guidance for:

* Vocabulary Engine
* Comprehension Engine
* AI Prompt Design
* Human Review
* Script Validation
* Obstacle Generation
* Runtime Read-Only Boundary

It must guide future P0 Analyze Engine work unless explicitly unfrozen.

========================================

1. Product Positioning
   ========================================

## External Positioning

## Marketing Layer

Video English Assistant is an AI-powered English learning assistant primarily designed for exam-oriented learners.

Primary users:

* Junior High
* Senior High
* CET-4
* CET-6
* TEM-4
* TEM-8
* GRE

The product helps learners:

1. Discover their English obstacles more quickly.
2. Learn English more efficiently.
3. Move beyond memorization toward genuine understanding and usage of English.

========================================
2. Internal Positioning
=======================

## Learning Engine Layer

The product is designed to improve examination performance by strengthening learners' real-world English comprehension and usage ability.

The product believes that examination performance and real-world language ability reinforce each other rather than compete with each other.

The goal is not simply to help learners memorize more words, but to help learners build genuine and transferable English comprehension and usage ability.

========================================
3. Product Goal
===============

Help English learners discover and solve their English obstacles more efficiently, improve examination performance, and gradually build transferable English comprehension and usage ability.

========================================
4. Core Learning Philosophy
===========================

Improving English performance comes primarily from improving language understanding ability rather than memorizing isolated knowledge points.

Memorization remains important, but understanding determines whether knowledge can be correctly recognized, transferred, and used.

The product therefore focuses on:

* Understanding
* Usage
* Transferable language ability
* Long-term language acquisition

instead of simple memorization alone.

========================================
5. Learner Level Contract
=========================

Learner levels are selected using familiar exam-based labels.

Supported levels:

* Junior High
* Senior High
* CET-4
* CET-6
* TEM-4
* TEM-8
* GRE

These levels are user-facing labels.

They are used as familiar references for learners to describe their current English proficiency.

They are not intended to reduce the learning engine to simple vocabulary-list lookup.

========================================
6. Obstacle Identification Principle
====================================

Obstacle identification is based on real-world English comprehension and usage difficulty for learners at that level.

The system does NOT simply ask:

"Is this word inside a particular exam vocabulary list?"

Instead, the system asks:

"For a learner at this level, which expressions are most likely to hinder comprehension, understanding, and usage of English?"

Representative real-world English materials include:

* Examination listening materials
* Reading passages
* Educational materials
* Videos
* Movies
* TV series
* TED talks
* News
* Interviews

These materials are examples rather than limitations of the learning engine.

========================================
7. Vocabulary Engine Guiding Principle
======================================

Vocabulary obstacles are determined according to the P0 Vocabulary Level Determination Contract.

The Vocabulary Engine uses a hierarchical decision system:

1. Frozen Vocabulary Lists
2. Expression Knowledge Base
3. Frequency Dictionaries
4. AI Assistance

Vocabulary obstacles are determined according to whether a word or expression is likely to hinder comprehension and usage for learners at the selected level.

Vocabulary Engine must not function as a simple vocabulary-list lookup system.

========================================
8. Comprehension Engine Guiding Principle
=========================================

Comprehension obstacles are determined according to whether the real meaning of an expression can be immediately understood by learners at the selected level.

Comprehension obstacles may exist even when every individual word is already known by the learner.

Priority recognition targets include:

* Fixed Expressions
* Collocations
* Slang
* Extended Meaning
* Culture-dependent Expressions
* Phrase Meaning
* Sentence Meaning
* Expressions whose individual words are known but whose combined meaning remains difficult to understand

The purpose of the Comprehension Engine is to identify understanding obstacles that directly affect:

* Reading
* Listening
* Expression
* Writing
* Examination performance
* Overall language ability

========================================
9. Product North Star Principle
===============================

Examination labels are used because they are familiar to learners.

Real-world language difficulty is used because it better represents what learners genuinely struggle to understand and use.

The product combines both:

Exam-oriented entry points
+
Real-world language understanding engines.

In short:

Product Entry:
Exam-based level system.

Product Goal:
Improve examination learning efficiency.

Core Capability:
Discover and solve real language obstacles that affect learners' comprehension and usage.

Long-Term Mission:
Help learners build transferable real English comprehension and usage ability.

========================================
10. Long-Term Product Mission
=============================

From memorizing English
to understanding English,
using English,
and ultimately thinking in English.
